# snapsme — App Description

## Tagline
Know where every dollar went. Without chasing anyone for a receipt.

## Short Description (one-liner)
snapsme is a lightweight expense tracker built for small teams — snap a photo or send a voice note, and AI logs the expense automatically, in real time, for everyone to see.

## Full Description

Running a small team means money moves fast — fuel, supplies, transport, small purchases — and keeping track of it usually means chasing receipts, reconstructing spend from memory, or hoping someone remembers to log it later. snapsme fixes that by making expense capture nearly effortless.

Snap a photo of a receipt, record a quick voice note, or send it straight from Telegram or WhatsApp — snapsme's AI reads the details automatically: vendor, amount, date, and category. Anything it isn't confident about gets flagged for a quick confirm, so the data stays accurate without extra typing.

Every submission lands instantly in a shared team feed. No more end-of-month reconstruction, no more asking "did anyone keep that receipt?" The owner sees exactly where money is going — by category, by team member, by vendor — in real time.

Built for how small teams actually spend: cash, company card, petty cash, or personal reimbursement, with no formal approval chain required. Works offline too, so a submission made with no signal syncs automatically the moment connectivity returns.

## Key Features

- **AI receipt capture** — photograph a receipt, get an itemized expense in seconds
- **Voice-note entry** — no receipt? No problem. Speak the expense and snapsme logs it
- **Telegram & WhatsApp intake** — submit expenses without opening the app at all
- **Real-time team feed** — see every submission across the team, live
- **Offline-first** — never lose a submission to a bad connection
- **Budgets & alerts** — set a limit per category, get a heads-up before it's exceeded
- **CSV export** — hand your accountant clean, exportable data in one tap
- **Built for how small teams spend** — cash, card, or petty cash, no rigid approval chain

## Who It's For

Small businesses with a handful of staff who spend on the company's behalf, and an owner who wants visibility without becoming a full-time bookkeeper.

## Positioning Note

snapsme is not an accounting suite — no invoicing, no payroll, no bank sync in v1. It does one thing well: getting every team expense captured the moment it happens, with almost no friction, so the owner always knows where the money went.
