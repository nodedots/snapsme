How it works

Three simple steps to eliminate lost paper receipts and manual bookkeeping for your business.

STEP 01

Snap a receipt or send a voice note

Staff snap a quick receipt photo or record a 5-second voice note directly on mobile or chat.

STEP 02

Automatic details reading

SnapSME reads the receipt for you — vendor, amount, and category — no typing required.

STEP 03

Lands in shared team list

It shows up in your team's shared list right away, so you always know what's been spent.
