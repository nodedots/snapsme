A Simple Monthly Expense Review Routine for Small Business Owners

A step-by-step 15-minute monthly review checklist to help founders and managers maintain financial control and prevent budget leakage.

The Power of a 15-Minute Monthly Review

Managing small business expenses doesn't require spending days trapped in accounting software. A focused, 15-minute monthly expense review is often all it takes to keep your team's spend under control and prevent cash leaks.

By setting aside a recurring time block on the 1st of every month, you can review transactions, identify spending trends, and ensure every expense is properly documented.

Step 1: Audit Unvouched or Pending Transactions (3 minutes)

Begin your review by checking for any pending or unconfirmed expense entries. Ensure every transaction has a corresponding receipt photo or voice note attached.

Prompt team members immediately regarding any missing details while the purchases are still fresh in their minds.

Step 2: Check Category Breakdown Against Budgets (5 minutes)

Review your category totals for the month. Compare actual spend against target budgets for your top categories (e.g. Fuel, Supplies, Meals, and Subscriptions).

Look for unusual spikes. For example, did fuel costs increase because of a new project, or is a vehicle in need of servicing?

Tip: Identify unexpected spending early so you can adjust operational plans before costs compound.

Step 3: Review Recurring Software and Vendor Subscriptions (4 minutes)

Small software subscriptions ($15/month, $29/month) add up quickly across a growing team. Use your monthly review to scan for unused app subscriptions, duplicate tools, or price increases.

Canceling just two unused software seats can save hundreds of dollars each year.

Step 4: Approve Reimbursements and Archive Monthly Feed (3 minutes)

Finalize all pending out-of-pocket reimbursements for your team and export or archive the monthly expense feed. Having clean, reconciled monthly records makes annual tax filing completely stress-free.

  • Verify all team reimbursement balances are paid in full.
  • Confirm every expense is assigned a clear category tag.
  • Archive the monthly log for tax readiness.

Build Financial Consistency

Consistency is the secret to stress-free business finances. When you pair real-time receipt capture with a quick monthly review, your business stays agile, profitable, and audit-ready.
