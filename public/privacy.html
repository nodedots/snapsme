Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026 • Version 2.0 • Applicable to SnapSME Web & Mobile Services

SnapSME ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of small business owners and their team members. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, encrypt, store, and safeguard financial transaction data, paper receipt media, voice recordings, and account information across the SnapSME platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide team expense and income tracking, SnapSME collects information in the following categories:

  • Account & Organization Information: Name, work email address, user ID (UID), photo URL (from Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth), workspace name, primary accounting currency, and assigned role (Owner or Staff).
  • Financial Transaction Records: Merchant/vendor names, transaction amounts, original currencies, converted base amounts, transaction dates, expense/income categories, money movement channels (Company Card, Reimbursement, Petty Cash, Supplier Direct), and user notes.
  • Receipt Media & Documents: Compressed image photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP) of physical paper receipts and digital document uploads (PDF, DOCX) attached to expense entries.
  • Voice Note Audio Transcripts: Transcribed text generated when team members record voice notes ("Paid $45 cash for fuel at Shell"). Audio recordings are processed in memory and transcribed text is retained with the entry.
  • Messaging Bot Tokens & Identifiers: Telegram user IDs, chat IDs, or WhatsApp phone numbers linked via the SnapSME Telegram / WhatsApp intake bots.

2. How We Process Your Financial & Receipt Data

We process your data strictly to operate and deliver core workspace features:

  • Extracting merchant names, amounts, currencies, and dates from uploaded receipt photos using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
  • Converting foreign currency transactions to your workspace base currency using live rates.
  • Rendering your team's real-time shared ledger feed and category budget progress.
  • Generating downloadable CSV accounting exports formatted for tax preparation and bookkeeping.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your business financial data or receipt records to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Offline Storage & Browser Local Memory

To enable offline receipt capture in dead zones without internet signal, SnapSME temporarily stores unsynced expense entries and active user session tokens in your browser's local storage (`localStorage` & `IndexedDB`). Once your connection is restored, pending entries sync automatically to the cloud and local queue state is cleared.

5. Data Retention & Workspace Deletion Rights

Your workspace expense and income records remain stored in your account as long as your workspace remains active. As the Workspace Owner, you have full authority to delete individual transactions at any time.

To request permanent deletion of your entire workspace and all associated receipt files and user records, submit a deletion request via our Contact Page. Workspace purges are completed within 48 hours.

6. GDPR, CCPA & User Privacy Rights

Under global data protection regulations (including General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act), users have the right to:

  • Access & Export: Export a full CSV dataset of all financial records at any time.
  • Rectification: Correct or update inaccurate transaction details or user profile info.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of personal and business financial data.
