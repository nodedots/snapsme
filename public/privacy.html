Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. What Data We Collect

SnapSME collects information necessary to provide team expense capture and tracking services for small businesses. This includes:

  • Account & Profile Info: Name, email address, phone number, workspace name, and assigned team roles (Owner or Member).
  • Expense Data: Merchant/vendor names, transaction amounts, dates, expense categories, notes, and payment methods.
  • Receipt Images & Voice Notes: Photo uploads of physical paper receipts and optional audio recordings submitted by team staff.
  • Chat Channel Identifiers: Telegram handles or WhatsApp phone numbers when staff link messaging channels for receipt submission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to operate, maintain, and improve the SnapSME service for your business workspace, including:

  • Processing paper receipt photos and voice notes into structured expense records.
  • Displaying real-time expense entries in your team's shared workspace feed.
  • Generating category budget summaries, monthly spend alerts, and CSV accounting exports.
  • Providing customer support and technical troubleshooting.

3. Third-Party Services Involved

SnapSME utilizes trusted third-party infrastructure and processing providers to deliver core features:

  • Google Firebase: Secure cloud storage, database hosting (Firestore), and web hosting.
  • Vision & AI Processing Providers: Optical character recognition (OCR) engines for receipt parsing.
  • Messaging Platforms: Telegram API and WhatsApp Business API for channel-based receipt intake.

4. Data Retention

Your workspace expense records and receipt media are stored for as long as your workspace account remains active. Business owners can export or request full deletion of workspace data at any time.

5. User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. Business owners can manage team member access and request full account export or deletion by contacting support.

6. Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via our Contact Page.
