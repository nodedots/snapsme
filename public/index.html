How it works

Three simple steps to eliminate lost paper receipts and manual bookkeeping for your business.

01

Snap a receipt or send a voice note

Staff snap a quick receipt photo or record a 5-second voice note directly on mobile or chat.

02

AI extracts vendor, amount, and category automatically

Multimodal AI parses vendor details, exact total, currency, and expense category with zero typing.

03

It lands in your team's shared feed instantly

Transactions appear live on the owner dashboard with duplicate detection and budget tracking.

Features built for small teams

Everything you need to track spending across 2 to 10 staff members effortlessly.

AI receipt capture

Extract vendor name, dollar total, taxes, and line items instantly from paper receipt photos.

Voice-note entry

Record brief audio notes on the go. AI transcribes and converts voice into structured expense records.

Telegram & WhatsApp intake

Staff submit expenses inside messaging apps they already use, requiring zero extra app downloads.

Offline-first sync

Capture receipts in cell dead zones. Transactions queue locally and sync automatically when reconnected.

Budgets & alerts

Set spend limits per category and receive flag notifications before team budgets are exceeded.

CSV export

Export clean, categorized transaction history with full audit logs for your accountant in one click.

Who it's for

SnapSME is designed specifically for small business owners managing 2 to 10 staff members who purchase supplies, fuel, meals, or equipment on behalf of the company. It gives owners complete real-time financial oversight without turning them into full-time bookkeepers.
