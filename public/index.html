Who snapsme is for

Designed specifically for small business owners managing 2 to 10 staff members who purchase supplies, fuel, meals, or equipment on behalf of the company. It gives owners complete real-time financial oversight without turning them into full-time accountants.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about snapsme, data security, accounting integrations, and offline access.

How is our team's spend data secured?

All transaction records and receipt photos are encrypted both in transit and at rest. We adhere strictly to data privacy standards and never sell or share your business's financial data.

How does it integrate with accounting software?

You can export clean, categorized expense histories as a CSV file in one click, perfectly formatted for QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, or your accountant's preferred spreadsheet.

Where is data stored & does it work offline?

snapsme is built offline-first. Expense records queue safely on your staff's device when out of cell service and sync automatically to your cloud workspace once reconnected.

Can I control staff permissions?

Yes. Workspace owners assign member roles. Staff members only see and edit their own submitted expenses, while owners and managers have full team visibility and budget alerts.
