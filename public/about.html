About SnapSME

Why SnapSME exists

Small business teams spend money constantly on everyday operational supplies, fuel, meals, and equipment, but rarely track it well. Most expense tools force business owners into becoming full-time bookkeepers just to figure out where cash went. SnapSME exists so an owner always knows where every dollar was spent in real-time, without complex setup or manual accounting software.

Free to use

SnapSME is free to use, and we intend to keep it that way for a long time. We believe small business owners deserve straightforward tools to manage their team spend without worrying about subscription tiers or hidden paywalls.

How it works

Your team snaps a photo of a paper receipt or records a quick voice note. SnapSME extracts the vendor, amount, and date instantly and posts it to a live, shared workspace feed for complete owner oversight.
