Getting Started

How do I create a workspace?

When you sign up, you will be guided through a quick 3-step onboarding setup to name your business, choose default expense categories, and invite your team. Once finished, your team workspace is instantly created.

How do I invite my team?

Go to the Team view in your workspace, click Add Member, and enter their name and email address or phone number. Invited members receive an invite to join your workspace feed immediately.

What if I run a business alone — do I need a team to use snapsme?

No, you can use SnapSME as a solo business owner to snap receipts, record voice notes, and track your business expenses without adding team members.

Can I choose default expense categories for my type of business?

Yes! During setup, you can select industry templates such as Retail, Services, Food & Beverage, Construction, or Freelance to pre-populate relevant expense categories and monthly budget limits.

Capturing Expenses & Income

How do I log my first sale or income entry?

Click + Add Income on your workspace feed (or use the camera icon or voice note tab). Enter the source name, amount, date, and optional notes — or snap an invoice photo — and your sale is posted instantly to the shared feed.

Can I import income transactions via CSV?

Yes! Under Import / Export, select Import CSV, switch to the Income tab, and upload your spreadsheet file. Map your columns for source, amount, and date to preview and bulk import your sales in seconds.

How does the Telegram & WhatsApp bot handle income vs. expenses?

When chatting with your linked bot, send /income to switch to income mode or /expense for expense mode. You can also text natural messages like "Received $500 from Acme Corp" or "Paid $45 for fuel", and the bot will categorize and log it to your workspace automatically.

How are income and expenses visually distinguished in the shared feed?

Expenses appear as torn-paper receipt cards with money movement tags and merchant badges. Income entries display a clean green left-border accent and an Income badge. Your dashboard header calculates Total Expenses, Total Income, and Net Cash Flow side-by-side.

How does receipt scanning work?

Snap a photo of your paper receipt or upload an image file. SnapSME reads the receipt image automatically to detect the merchant name, date, total amount, and category for instant saving.

What if I don't have a physical receipt?

If you don't have a paper receipt, click Voice Note to speak what you spent (e.g., "Paid $45 for fuel at Shell"), or enter the vendor and amount manually in seconds.

Can I submit expenses without opening the app?

Yes! Staff can send receipt photos or voice recordings directly via Telegram or WhatsApp bot integrations connected to your workspace.

What happens if the AI gets a detail wrong?

You can review and edit any extracted field (vendor, amount, date, category) before confirming, or edit the entry at any time from the feed.

How does snapsme know how money was spent (cash, card, etc.)?

SnapSME detects payment method keywords (e.g. Visa, Cash, Debit) on receipt images or allows staff to select their payment method during manual or voice note entry.

Team & Roles

What's the difference between an owner and a staff member?

Workspace owners can manage settings, budgets, member invitations, and CSV data exports. Staff members can capture, view, and manage expenses across the team feed.

Can I remove someone from my workspace?

Yes, workspace owners can remove team members at any time from the Team view by clicking the action menu next to their profile.

Can staff see each other's submissions?

Yes, all submitted expenses appear in the shared workspace activity feed so team members and owners have real-time visibility into company spending.

Does snapsme require approval before an expense counts?

By default, submitted expenses are logged immediately to keep bookkeeping frictionless. Flagged expenses (such as potential duplicates) can be reviewed by the owner.

Offline & Sync

Does snapsme work without an internet connection?

Yes, SnapSME is built offline-first. You can snap receipts or record voice notes in remote areas or dead zones with no active signal.

What happens to an expense I submit while offline?

Expenses submitted offline are stored securely on your device and automatically sync to your workspace feed once internet connection is restored.

Dashboard & Budgets

How do I set a budget for a category?

Workspace owners can open Settings to assign monthly target budget limits for each expense category (e.g. Fuel, Office Supplies).

What happens when I go over budget?

When category spending approaches or exceeds your monthly target, visual warning badges and budget cards on your dashboard highlight the over-budget categories.

Can I customize what I see on my dashboard?

Yes! In Settings under Dashboard Preferences, you can toggle cards like Top Vendor, Team Leaderboard, Budget vs. Actual, and Spend by Day.

Can I export my data?

Yes, workspace owners can click Export CSV at any time to download a complete spreadsheet of expenses for accounting or tax preparation.

Branding

Can I add my business logo?

Yes! Under Brand Basics in Settings, owners can upload a custom logo image to display on their workspace header and reports.

Can I change the app's colors to match my brand?

Yes, owners can enter a custom brand accent color hex code (default `#0f7a52`) in Settings to customize key action buttons across their app.

Account & Security

How do I sign in?

Click Sign in on the header navigation bar to log in using your work email address and password or your Google account.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Click Forgot password? on the sign-in modal, enter your account email address, and follow the password reset link sent to your inbox.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all workspace data and uploaded receipt images are stored securely on Google Firebase cloud infrastructure with strict access controls.

How do I sign out?

Click your user avatar in the top right corner of the header navigation bar and select Sign out from the dropdown menu.
