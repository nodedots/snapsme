Storage Disclosure

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026  ·  Version 2.0  ·  SnapSME Storage Disclosures

SnapSME ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies, browser local storage (localStorage), and session storage to deliver an offline-first financial recording application. This policy explains what browser storage technologies we use, why they are essential for offline queueing, and how you can manage your browser preferences.

1. What Are Cookies & Browser Local Storage

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a website. Local Storage (localStorage) is a secure browser memory partition that allows modern web applications to store structured data locally on your device without transmitting it to external tracking servers.

2. How SnapSME Uses Storage Technologies

SnapSME relies on essential local storage keys and session tokens to provide seamless offline capture and security:

Storage Key / Cookie Type Purpose & Function
Firebase Auth Session Token Essential Cookie Maintains your authenticated session so you stay signed in to your workspace.
snapsme_current_user Local Storage Stores active user profile info (name, role, business ID) for instant rendering without network requests.
snapsme_offline_queue Local Storage Holds unsynced expense entries captured offline until cellular or Wi-Fi connection is restored.
snapsme_onboarding_completed Local Storage Remembers workspace onboarding status to prevent repeated onboarding prompts on login.
snapsme_workspace Local Storage Caches your active workspace context (name, currency, settings) for fast offline access.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising Cookies

SnapSME does not use third-party cross-site advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling tools. Optional usage telemetry (Google Analytics for Firebase) is used strictly to monitor app reliability, page load performance, and error crash logs — never for advertising purposes.

4. How to Manage or Clear Local Storage

You can manage, inspect, or clear local storage and cookies anytime through your browser's developer tools or security settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies & site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
Note: Clearing local storage while offline will sign you out of your account and clear any unsynced offline receipt drafts currently queued on your device.
