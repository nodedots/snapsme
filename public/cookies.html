Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage

Cookies and local storage are small text files or data structures stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your session, preferences, and offline queue status.

2. How SnapSME Uses Cookies & Local Storage

SnapSME uses essential local storage and session tokens to provide seamless offline-first performance and user authentication:

  • Authentication Sessions (Firebase Auth): Secure session tokens that keep you logged in to your business workspace across browser reloads.
  • Offline Queue & Storage: Local browser storage (`localStorage`) that temporarily saves receipt entries when captured in dead zones without cell signal.
  • User Preferences: Storing active view settings, offline mode toggles, and currency display preferences.

3. How to Manage or Clear Cookies

You can control or clear cookies and local storage directly through your browser settings. Please note that clearing local storage will log you out of your workspace and reset any unsynced offline receipt drafts.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions about our use of cookies or local storage, please reach out via our Contact Page.
