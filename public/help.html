Help Center & FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about using SnapSME for your team.

How do I invite my team?

As a business Workspace Owner, you can invite team members (staff) during the initial onboarding setup or anytime from the Team tab in your workspace dashboard. Simply enter their name along with their email address or phone number. Invited members receive instant access to submit expenses on behalf of your company.

What happens if I don't have a receipt?

No problem! If a merchant doesn't provide a paper receipt, staff can use the Voice Note capture mode to speak what they spent (e.g. "Paid $28 for lunch meeting at City Diner"), or submit a quick manual entry with the vendor name and amount. SnapSME converts voice recordings directly into categorized expense records.

Does SnapSME work offline?

Yes! SnapSME is built offline-first. You can snap receipt photos or record voice notes in cell dead zones, basements, or remote jobsites with no internet connection. Transactions queue safely on your device and automatically sync to your workspace feed once you reconnect to Wi-Fi or cellular data.

How do I connect Telegram or WhatsApp?

From the Settings page, navigate to the Integrations & Messaging Bots section. Grab your dedicated Telegram bot link or WhatsApp webhook code and share it with your staff. Team members can then snap receipt photos directly inside their favorite messaging app without installing extra software.

How do I export my data?

You can export your workspace transaction history in one click by clicking the Export CSV button on either the Shared Feed or the Dashboard page. The exported spreadsheet includes dates, merchants, amounts, categories, staff submitter names, and money movement tags formatted for easy handoff to your accountant.

Is my data secure?

All expense entries and receipt images are stored securely on Google Firebase cloud infrastructure with strict access controls. Only authorized members of your business workspace can access your expense feed.
