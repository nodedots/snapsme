Getting Started & Workspace Management

How do I create a workspace and invite my team?

Click Create your workspace on the landing page or sign in to launch onboarding. You'll enter your business name, choose your primary accounting currency, and set up custom expense categories. To invite staff, enter their email address or phone number under the Team Members step or manage them anytime from the Team tab inside your dashboard.

What roles exist and what can each team member do?

SnapSME supports two primary user roles:
  • Owner / Administrator: Has complete administrative authority. Can configure workspace settings, manage team permissions, view all ledger records, delete entries, and export CSV reports.
  • Staff Member: Can snap paper receipts, record voice notes, log manual entries, and view the team's shared activity feed — but cannot alter workspace settings or remove entries.

Is SnapSME free for small businesses?

Yes! SnapSME is free for small business teams of 2 to 10 staff members. There are no credit card requirements, no hidden fees, and no trial timeouts. You get full access to photo receipt scanning, voice processing, multi-currency conversion, offline queueing, CSV export, and messaging bot integration.

Receipt Scanning & Voice Capture

What file formats can I upload for paper receipts and invoices?

SnapSME accepts all standard document formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP image photos (captured directly via mobile camera or uploaded), digital PDF receipts and invoices, and DOCX/DOC Word documents. Images are automatically compressed client-side before storage to minimize bandwidth.

How does automatic receipt scanning and AI extraction work?

When a staff member snaps a photo or uploads a document, SnapSME runs a multi-tier OCR engine that automatically parses and populates the Merchant Name, Transaction Amount, Currency, Date, Category, Money Movement Channel, and Context Notes. All fields are pre-filled so staff can review and save in seconds.

Does SnapSME work offline in dead zones without internet signal?

Yes! SnapSME is built offline-first. When staff capture receipts or voice entries in basements, remote jobsites, or transit dead zones, entries are stored safely in local browser memory (`localStorage`). Once cellular connection or Wi-Fi is restored, transactions sync automatically with your cloud workspace feed.

Income Tracking & Cash Flow

Can I log business income as well as expenses?

Yes! You can log income by scanning bank deposit slips, customer payment receipts, or sales invoices, recording voice notes ("Received $1,200 cash from Acme Corp for order #402"), or logging manual income entries. Income is tracked separately from expenses, giving you a live view of net cash flow (Income minus Expenses).

How does multi-currency conversion work for global transactions?

SnapSME supports 140+ world currencies (USD, NGN, EUR, GBP, KES, CAD, ZAR, etc.). When an expense or income entry is captured in a foreign currency, SnapSME fetches live exchange rates via the ExchangeRate API and records both the original foreign amount and the converted base amount in your workspace default accounting currency.

Telegram & WhatsApp Bot Setup

How do I connect our Telegram or WhatsApp bot for instant receipt submission?

Read our step-by-step publishing guide in the Bot Integration Guide. You create a Telegram bot via @BotFather or register a WhatsApp Cloud API webhook, obtain your Secret Token, and link your workspace using the /link [code] command.

Data Export, Security & Privacy

How do I export CSV reports for tax filings or accounting?

Click the Export CSV button at the top of the Shared Feed or Dashboard. The generated spreadsheet includes all transaction records (expenses and income), transaction dates, merchants, original & converted amounts, currencies, categories, payment channels, submitters, and notes — formatted for QuickBooks, Xero, Excel, or tax accountants.

Is our financial data private and secure?

Yes. All workspace data is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS and stored on enterprise-grade Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure with strict Firestore Security Rules. Only authenticated members of your business workspace can read or submit records. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
