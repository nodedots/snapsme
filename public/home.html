How it works

Three simple steps to eliminate lost paper receipts and manual bookkeeping for your business.

01

Snap a receipt or send a voice note

Staff snap a quick receipt photo or record a 5-second voice note directly on mobile or chat.

02

Automatic receipt details reading

03

Lands in your team's shared list

Features built for small teams

Everything you need to track spending across 2 to 10 staff members effortlessly.

Receipt photo capture

Snap a photo. We read the vendor, the total, and the category for you.

Voice-note entry

No receipt on hand? Just say what you spent — snapsme turns it into a logged expense.

Send via Telegram & WhatsApp

Staff can text receipt photos or voice notes in the chat apps they already use — no new apps to install.

Works without internet

Snap receipts even with no cell signal. Everything saves on your phone and updates automatically once you're back online.

Budgets & alerts

Who it's for

You shouldn't have to become a bookkeeper to know where your team's money went. SnapSME is designed specifically for small business owners managing 2 to 10 staff members who purchase supplies, fuel, meals, or equipment on behalf of the company. It gives owners complete oversight without the headache of manual tracking.
